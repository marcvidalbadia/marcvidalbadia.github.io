Marc Vidal

PhD/MSc in Mathematical and Applied Statistics · PhD in Art Science

Research Associate, Ghent University   •   Guest researcher, Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences

My research focuses on mathematical and methodological statistics for functional and high-dimensional data, with particular interests in statistical representation, latent structure, and discrimination. I work on operator-based methods, dimensionality reduction, covariance structure models, and discrete functional data analysis. A central theme of my recent research is how finite-dimensional representations retain or alter statistical information present in an underlying infinite-dimensional model. Neurophysiological and neuroimaging data provide a recurring application setting for this work.
